About

Rogue Conflict was my first and so far only full game I've made. It took me almost 3 full years to create, and I learnt so much during that time, way too much for me to fit into a desription of this size. The goal of the game from the onset was to make a wargame roguelike, the first of its kind. A game takes the mechanics from games like Unity of command or Order of battle, and placing them within the structure of a roguelike game such as Slay the spire or Into the breach.

During the games creation I learnt so much. Everything from how to draw 2D art, to how to code complex algorithms, how to make procedurally generated maps, enemy AI systems, balancing, playtesting, marketing, composing music, and much much more. I hope to one day write a full blog post of my experience making this game, so I can go into the full details of it. But suffice to say, it was a massive learning experience in game dev.
