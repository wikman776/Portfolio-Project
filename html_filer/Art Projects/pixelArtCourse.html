About

This a course I took on how to draw pixel art for games. The reason for taking this course is ofcourse that my own game Rogue Conflict would be using that art style.

And the reason for going with pixel art as my choice of styling for my game, is because one, I really like the look of it and many of my favourite games estethically speaking uses it, like fore example Red Alert 2, which is one of my favourite looking games. But another reason for going with pixel art for my game, is that I thought that since I was new to drawing in general, that pixel art would be a good choice, because it is a bit simpler I feel like than drawing fully rendered art assets.
